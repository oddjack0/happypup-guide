How these guides work

Every guide uses reward-based methods: mark and reward the behaviour you want, manage the environment so mistakes are hard to make, and keep sessions short and frequent. You will find realistic timelines rather than overnight fixes, because dogs learn at the speed of repetition — and honest pointers to a vet or a certified professional trainer whenever a problem is bigger than a web page.

Please read: HappyPup Guide provides general information about dog training and everyday care for educational purposes only. It is not veterinary advice and is not a substitute for a veterinarian, a certified professional dog trainer, or a veterinary behaviourist. If your dog is ill, injured, in pain, or showing aggression, or if a behaviour problem is severe or getting worse, please contact a qualified professional promptly.
